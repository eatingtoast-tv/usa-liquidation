Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how USA Liquidation (operated by US Furniture Incorporated, "we," "us") collects and uses your information when you visit our website, fill out a form, chat with us, or text or call us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us — your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Details about what you're interested in — for example, the trailer size, food type, and budget on our quote form, or the item you ask about.
  • Messages you send us by text, chat, or email.
  • Basic website data such as browser type and pages visited.

How We Use It

  • To answer your questions and send you quotes.
  • To follow up about your request by call, text, or email.
  • To send occasional updates and offers, if you agreed to receive them.
  • To run and improve our business and website.

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you check the consent box on our forms or opt in through our website chat, you agree to receive calls and text messages from USA Liquidation about your request, quotes, and occasional offers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to any third party. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run our business (for example, our customer messaging and website tools), and only so they can provide those services to us — or when required by law.

Keeping Your Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data is 100% secure, but we work to keep your information private.

Your Choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, or ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us below.
